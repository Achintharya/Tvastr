QUALITY TOOLS

Industry-Standard Quality Frameworks

Built-in support for proven manufacturing quality methodologies

🔬 Statistical Process Control (SPC)

Real-time monitoring: X̄ control charts, CUSUM tracking, Western Electric rules for pattern detection. Cpk and Ppk process capability indices with sigma level analysis.

🐟 Fishbone Diagrams (Ishikawa)

Root cause analysis: Systematic investigation using 6M categories (Materials, Methods, Machines, Manpower, Measurements, Environment) with defect correlation.

⚠️ FMEA (Failure Mode & Effects Analysis)

Risk prioritization: Systematic identification and scoring of failure modes with RPN calculation (Severity × Occurrence × Detection). Focus on high-impact issues first.

📊 Pareto Analysis

80/20 principle: Identify the vital few defect types causing majority of quality issues. Data-driven prioritization for improvement efforts and resource allocation.

🔍 Defect Fingerprinting

Pattern tracking: DBSCAN clustering identifies recurring defect patterns across heats, molds, and shifts. Links to specific manufacturing conditions for targeted corrective action.

💰 Cost of Quality

Financial impact: Track prevention, appraisal, internal failure, and external failure costs. Quantify quality improvement ROI and justify investment decisions.
